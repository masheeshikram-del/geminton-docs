Subscription (Geminton Pro)

How do I subscribe to Geminton Pro?

Menu → About → Upgrade to Pro (or tap any Pro badge) → Start free trial → confirm in Google Play or the App Store.

Is there a free trial? When am I charged?

  • 3-month free trial for new subscribers—you pay nothing during the trial.
  • After that, monthly renewal at the store price unless you cancel before the trial ends.

How do I cancel or unsubscribe?

Geminton cannot cancel for you—use the store:

  • In app: About → Manage or cancel subscription
  • Android: Play Store → Subscriptions → Geminton → Cancel
  • iPhone: Settings → Subscriptions → Geminton → Cancel

Uninstalling does not stop billing.

I reinstalled—how do I get Pro back?

About → Upgrade to Pro → Restore purchases with the same store account. This restores Pro only—not your gem records (use backup for data).

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

  • Free: Gem Library, add and edit records, exhibition import/export, QR scan, and logging buyer interest at exhibitions. Configure is view-only.
  • Pro: Full Configure, Analytics, Message Templates, Import Data, automatic backup (where supported), certificate scan/upload, and Buyer Interests follow-up.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone first. Optional backup in Configure can copy it to the cloud (for example Google Drive on Android, or iCloud Drive on iPhone). Geminton does not keep a copy of your gem records on its own servers.

How do I back up and restore?

Use a backup ZIP (Configure → Backup). It includes your records and photos, payment history, templates, and settings. An Excel export alone has no photos. To restore, open Configure → Backup → Restore on the new or reinstalled device and pick the newest backup you trust.

What does “certificate scan” do?

A Pro feature that reads text from a certificate photo using a secure cloud scan and suggests field values. Fair-use daily limits apply, and you can always enter details manually. Always check suggested values before you rely on them.

Will importing the same Excel file create duplicates?

No. Geminton matches stones by gem type, carat, record name, and first certificate, and updates existing rows instead of adding copies. The import preview shows how many will be created or updated.

Can I find more help inside the app?

Yes. Open Menu → Help for the user guide and FAQ, or tap the chat button at the bottom right to ask Geminton how to do something.
